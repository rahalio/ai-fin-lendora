# Lendora — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Head of digital lending

- As a head of digital lending, I want a high straight-through accept rate inside risk appetite, so that we meet the five-minute promise without a credit-officer bottleneck.
- As a head of digital lending, I want funnel metrics by segment, so that we see where policy—not UX—blocks conversion.

### Chief credit officer

- As a CCO, I want policy overlays that can hard-veto the model, so that AI cannot outrun appetite.
- As a CCO, I want early-warning dashboards on new vintages, so that we catch deterioration before charge-off.

### Credit underwriter

- As an underwriter, I want refer cases with top factors and documents the model used, so that I decide quickly.
- As an underwriter, I want to record override reasons, so that policy and model teams learn from exceptions.

### Model risk officer

- As a model-risk officer, I want challenger stability and discrimination metrics before promotion, so that we do not ship fragile lifts.
- As a model-risk officer, I want immutable decision reproductions, so that audits do not depend on tribal knowledge.

### Digital product manager

- As a product manager, I want clear decline reason taxonomy for UX messaging, so that applicants know what to fix when allowed.

### Platform administrator

- As an administrator, I want product-level policy packs, so that personal loans and SME products do not share unsafe defaults.
- As an administrator, I want access controls separating policy editors from model operators, so that neither can unilaterally loosen credit.
